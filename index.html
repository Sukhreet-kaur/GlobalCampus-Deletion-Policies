Account & Data Deletion Request

At GlobalCampus, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from our systems, please follow the simple steps below.

How to request account deletion

Send an email from your registered email address to our support team at:
sukhreetkaur2704@gmail.com

What data is deleted?

  • Your account credentials and profile information (Name, Email, Mobile Number, Country).
  • User session history and authentication tokens.
  • Any personal data stored in our Firebase database associated with your User ID.

Data Retention & Processing Time

Once we receive your deletion request via email, your account and data will be permanently removed from our active database within 48 to 72 hours.

Need Help?

If you have any questions or encounter issues regarding your data privacy, feel free to reach out to us at sukhreetkaur2704@gmail.com.
